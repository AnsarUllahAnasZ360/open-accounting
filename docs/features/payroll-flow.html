OpenBooks · Payroll guide

How a payroll run flows — HR to “Paid”

OpenBooks payroll is a register, not a processor — you pay people your own way, and the books stay correct. It uses maker-checker: HR prepares the run, and an Owner or Accountant approves it and marks it paid. Nothing hits your ledger until it’s approved.

Who does what

HR · the Preparer

Prepares the payroll

  • Manages the employee roster (add, edit, terminate, bulk-import).
  • Generates each month’s run from active salaries.
  • Adds a one-time Bonus or Deduction per person.
  • Clicks Submit for approval to hand it off.
  • Cannot approve, post to the ledger, or mark paid.
Owner / Accountant · the Approver

Approves & pays

  • Reviews the submitted run and Approves & posts it to the ledger.
  • Or Sends it back with a required reason for HR to fix.
  • Marks it paid once the money leaves the bank.
  • Has full preparer rights too (can do everything HR can).

HR is never pinged about their own action; approvers are notified when a run is submitted.

Two ways it gets marked paid

Manual

“Mark all paid”

The approver clicks Mark all paid. OpenBooks settles every line and, if a matching bank outflow exists, links it — so the same money is never double-counted. Works even without a connected bank.

Automatic

Auto-settle on bank sync

When a bank payment that clearly matches an approved run syncs in — exact amount, unique, within ±5 days, and labelled payroll (Gusto/Wise/ADP…) — the run settles itself, no click. Approvers get a “payroll auto-settled” notification.

How you verify it’s actually paid

Three signals, all pointing at the same fact: ① the Payroll Payable account clears to $0 (a leftover balance = someone’s still unpaid) · ② a real bank transaction is matched to the run (money genuinely left your account) · ③ the run status shows Paid and the “unmatched” count drops to zero.

How each line is calculated

Each line is Base + Bonus − Deduction. Salaries are stored and paid in the employee’s own currency, and OpenBooks also shows the USD equivalent for reporting.

The short version

HR drafts the month and hits Submit → the Owner/Accountant Approves & posts (books the expense + a payable) → the bank payment is matched (manually or automatically) → the payable clears to $0 and the run is Paid. Every dollar is counted once, and nothing posts without an approver.
Employees don’t log in — they receive payslips by email or a secure download link. Roles: owner / accountant / hr. Ledger accounts: 5000 Payroll Expense, 2200 Payroll Payable, 4200 / 6999 for FX gain / loss.
